TL;DR: In order to imitate you, Korbwalker stores your messages. If you ever want to, you can always make Korbwalker stop doing that or see and delete what it has collected.

Korbwalker's main functionality is to imitate users. To do this, Korbwalker collects and stores the text contents of the messages you send in select channels. When Korbwalker receives a command to imitate you, your stored messages are processed through an algorithm made to generate new sentences that sound like you. The text content of the messages Korbwalker collects are kept by Korbwalker's owner and operator, [@korbosoft](https://github.com/korbosoft/), indefinitely. [@korbosoft](https://github.com/korbosoft/) reserves the right to erase part or all of your stored data at any time if it is found to be in violation of the rules/laws of any institution that governs the way Korbwalker functions — that is:
- Discord's ToS
- the rules of any Discord server that Korbwalker is in
- US law (the country Korbo is based in)

When you register your Discord user ID with Korbwalker, Korbwalker will begin to collect all the messages you send from that point on in the public channels in all servers that Korbwalker is a member of until you decide to unregister. Korbwalker stores the following parts of each message:
- text (including mentions, emojis, and URLs to sites, images, and videos)
- time and date the message was posted
- your Discord user ID

You, at any time, have the right to:
1. download a copy of your data.
2. delete your data.
3. remove your registration from Korbwalker. Note that unregistering does not automatically delete your data.
